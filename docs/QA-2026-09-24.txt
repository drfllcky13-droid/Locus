Open problems and questions for Addison
---------------------------------------
1. Mesh evidence: OBJ, glTF/GLB and PLY meshes import and are hashed, but nothing shows or
   uses them (the 3D view draws point clouds only; the scene builder places library models
   only). Should imported meshes be viewable, or placeable in the 3D scene?
2. Unsaved analysis work: picks and stains in the trajectory, bloodstain and camera tools are
   held only in the panel until "Save analysis"; closing the window or opening another
   project discards them without asking. Ask before closing, keep drafts, or leave as is?
3. The case report lists evidence, measurements, diagrams, scenes, registrations and
   analyses, but not point-cloud cleanups (they are in the audit log). Should it?
4. Trajectory: a click inside a hole in a thick panel can fit the far face's rim, so a
   surface's entry and exit come out swapped in the report's point table (direction
   unaffected). Proposal: warn when an "entry" lies beyond its "exit" along the fitted path.
5. Witness view: the standing point isn't checked to be on a level surface (a wall click is
   accepted as "the floor").
6. Small UI items, left as they are: a lasso that encloses nothing still opens the delete
   dialog (0 points); "Outliers: 8 neighbours, beyond [8] [2] σ" repeats the count; the
   sidebar's audit-entry count refreshes only on import, verify or reopen; a scene extrusion
   doesn't say when its diagram has a newer revision; a crash tool with a required input
   missing shows nothing rather than what is missing; the import dialog can't be cancelled
   while reading; a measurement's × deletes it at once with no undo in the UI (the record is
   kept, marked deleted, and logged).
7. Not app defects: several older driver scripts in E:\Claude\scratch\locus (builders_ui,
   crash_ui's crush step, volume_ui, package_ui) no longer match the UI (renamed viewer,
   new selects, a renamed command argument). The qa\ copies are current.
